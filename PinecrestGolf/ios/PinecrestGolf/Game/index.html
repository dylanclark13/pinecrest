 WELCOME TO PINECREST

Find your swing.

144 holes across eight courses, from sheltered parkland to demanding coastal links. Choose a front nine, back nine, or full round from Courses. Get the ball into each cup in as few strokes as possible.

01
Line it up

Click the course or overhead map to aim. Use ← and → for small adjustments.

02
Choose your club

Use the club arrows to browse all 14 clubs. Number keys 1–8 select the first eight. Your golfer changes clubs too. Woods, irons, wedges, and the putter have different lengths, heads, and swings.

03
Power. Timing. Follow-through.

Hold the swing button or Space for power, then release. Tap again when the timing marker crosses the center. Early contact hooks the ball; late contact slices it. The ball leaves the club at impact.

04
Spin and course strategy

Use Spin beside your club to set backspin, topspin, draw, or fade before swinging. Gusts bend shots. Deep rough and bunkers reduce carry. Press G to see the green grid: moving dots travel downhill, faster on steeper slopes. Fast putts can run across the cup. Water and out-of-bounds add one penalty stroke and return you to your last lie.

C changes camera · G shows green slopes · Esc cancels setup
Earn tokens by completing holes. Spend them on individual club upgrades in the clubhouse. The range guide excludes wind, spin, and elevation.
