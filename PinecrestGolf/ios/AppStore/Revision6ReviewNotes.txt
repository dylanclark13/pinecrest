Review notes for revision 6. Verify these steps on the final uploaded build before submission.

This revision opens a native SwiftUI clubhouse with course selection, equipment upgrades, statistics and settings. The game engine and eight courses are bundled inside the application. Device career gameplay and practice do not require a website connection or an account.

Suggested review steps:
1. Launch without internet. In Courses, choose a course and start a round.
2. Return to Clubhouse and try Driving range and Putting green. Practice does not change career records.
3. Finish a hole, then open Equipment to see tokens and exact distance, forgiveness and spin comparisons for each upgrade. Purchases require tokens earned from play.
4. Open Stats to see fairways, greens, putts per hole and best full rounds.
5. Close and reopen between shots, then choose Resume saved round. Device saves include current hole, strokes and ball position. An interrupted in-flight shot resumes from the previous saved position.
6. A paired extended controller supports aiming, swings, clubs, slope dots and camera. Settings lists the controls. Supported iPhones provide native impact and hole completion feedback.

Settings > Online career retains access to existing website accounts. This optional mode requires internet. Its progress is separate from the default device career. There is no automatic synchronization.

No account or credentials are needed for the default offline experience. Challenge courses open sequentially by scoring 90 or lower over 18 holes, beginning at Atlantic Links.
