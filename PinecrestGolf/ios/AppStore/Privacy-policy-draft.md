# Privacy policy working notes, not a published policy

The iPhone testing build connects to the public Pinecrest Golf website. The previous offline-only privacy description does not apply.

Known data: optional email accounts, password hashes, session identifiers, guest identifiers, golfer display names and appearance, saved rounds, hole scores, tokens and club upgrades. Public course records display golfer names and scores. The iPhone container uses persistent website cookies to retain sessions. Its native code does not add advertising or analytics SDKs.

Before publication, the operator must provide their legal identity, monitored contact address, data retention and deletion details, hosting-provider disclosures and any relevant regional terms. Implement and describe account deletion. Verify hosting infrastructure collection and logging rather than claiming no data collection.
